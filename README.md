### Hi, I'm Jon 👋

I'm a staff product designer and design engineer. I've spent 25 years making things for the web, and I split my time between Jersey and Bordeaux.

I design experiences that reveal the value hidden inside complex products or services, from research to release. Most of that work is in **RegTech**, **EventTech** and **PropTech**, where the product has to work for the business, the regulator and the customer all at once. I work across the strategy, the journeys, the design system and the code that ships it. I build what I design, so nothing gets watered down in a handover.

#### Recent work

**[White Paper](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/white-paper-booking-engine-design-development)** · EventTech
Consolidated legacy systems into one booking platform and back office. 90% of bookings now happen online, booking admin is down 75% and checkout takes 55% fewer clicks. The back office went from four people to one.

**[Vaiie Identify](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/vaiie-identify-product-design)** · RegTech
Redesigned the identity verification journey around its sticking points and unhappy paths. Successful verifications went up 4x without changing the vendor's technology, and Vaiie became a government-approved digital identity platform.

**Vaiie Onboard** · RegTech
Onboarding for complex ownership structures. My working HTML prototype became the project's single source of truth and helped secure internal investment from Jersey Post.

**[StreetPal](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/streetpal-ios-app-design-development)** · iOS
A native walking app for street photographers, built in Swift with MapKit, WeatherKit and HealthKit.
