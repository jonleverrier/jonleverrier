### Hi, I'm Jon 👋

I'm a freelance, AI-native product designer who also writes the code. I live between Jersey and Bordeaux.

I design experiences that reveal the value hidden inside complex products or services, from research to release. Then I build what I've designed, so nothing gets watered down in a handover.

Most of my work is in **RegTech**, **EventTech** and **PropTech**.

#### What I'm working on

- **[White Paper](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/white-paper-booking-engine-design-development)**: a conference booking engine I designed and built. It cut booking admin by 75%.
- **[Vaiie](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/vaiie-identify-product-design)**: product design for identity verification. Successful verifications went up 4x.
- **[StreetPal](https://jonleverrier.com/case-study/streetpal-ios-app-design-development)**: an iOS companion app for street photographers. Designed and built by me.

#### What I do

- **Research**: competitor analysis, personas, journey mapping, and turning ambitions into goals and KPIs you can measure
- **Design**: information architecture, wireframes, UX/UI, prototyping, AI context design, and conversation, trust and consent design
- **Development**: front-end, design systems, Craft CMS, DevOps, technical SEO/AEO, and automating the manual stuff
- **Branding**: logos, style guides and AI tone of voice

Over the years I've worked with HSBC International, Lloyds Bank International, the Government of Jersey, Jersey Post, TV5Monde, Feel Unique and Sure Telecom, among others.
