### Hi, I'm Jon 👋

I'm a staff product designer and design engineer. I've spent 25 years making things for the web, and I split my time between Jersey and Bordeaux.

I design experiences that reveal the value hidden inside complex products or services, from research to release. Most of that work is in regulated or operationally heavy areas: **RegTech**, **EventTech** and **PropTech**. In those fields the product has to work for the business, the regulator and the customer all at once.

I work at the level of the whole system: the strategy, the journeys, the design system and the code that ships it. I build what I design, so nothing gets watered down in a handover.

#### How I work

- **Strategy first.** I turn ambitions into goals and KPIs you can measure before anything gets drawn.
- **Design for the unhappy path.** Failed verifications and edge cases shouldn't end the journey.
- **Prototypes as the source of truth.** Working code settles debates that static mockups can't.
- **Systems over screens.** I build design systems and component libraries that outlast the project.
- **Accessibility as a baseline.** Semantic HTML, WCAG AA contrast and reduced motion are part of the job, not extras.
- **AI-native, judgement-led.** I use AI for research, analysis and prototyping. The design decisions stay mine.
